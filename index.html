Simple launch plan

From idea to first customer

Use this four-step process instead of spending money before you know what people will pay for.

DAY 1 · CHOOSE

Pick one service

Choose something you can already do or learn with free tutorials. Keep the offer specific.

DAY 2 · SHOW

Create 3 samples

Make example work for imaginary brands. Clearly label samples and don't claim fake clients.

DAY 3 · REACH OUT

Contact real prospects

Send polite, personalized messages to suitable businesses. Respect platform rules and opt-outs.

DAY 4+ · DELIVER

Earn trust first

Agree on scope and payment before work. Deliver on time, request feedback, and improve your offer.
